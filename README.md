## 🧑‍💻 Vibe coding & AI coding

1. [Cursor](https://cursor.com) ⭐ **Best pick**: Editing a real codebase with an AI pair programmer. *Free plan + paid.*
2. [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code): Agentic coding from your terminal. *Paid.*
3. [Lovable](https://lovable.dev): Prompt-to-full-stack web app, no setup. *Free plan + paid.*
4. [Bolt.new](https://bolt.new): Spinning up and deploying web apps in the browser. *Free plan + paid.*
5. [Replit](https://replit.com): Building and hosting apps with Replit Agent. *Free plan + paid.*
6. [v0](https://v0.app): Generating React/Next.js UI from prompts. *Free plan + paid.*
7. [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot): Inline completions in VS Code & JetBrains. *Free plan + paid.*
8. [Windsurf](https://devin.ai/desktop): AI-native IDE alternative to Cursor (now renamed Devin Desktop by Cognition). *Free plan + paid.*
9. [Faylee](https://faylee.app): One-link bug reports (video, console, network, steps) that Claude Code, Codex or Cursor can read over MCP and fix; free for up to 3 members. *Free plan + paid.*
10. [Backenly](https://backenly.com): Backend (Postgres, auth, storage, realtime) your coding agent builds over MCP, with self-healing and human approval for risky changes; free plan. *Free plan + paid.*
11. [MAO](https://trymao.dev): Desktop canvas to run Claude Code, Codex, Grok and other agents side by side; 7-day free trial, then $20 one-time. *Paid.*
12. [AC Bridge](https://www.ac-bridge.com): Turns your smart lights and plugs into status lights for Claude Code and Codex; 7-day free trial, then £2.99/month. *Paid.*

## 🤖 AI agents & assistants

1. [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) ⭐ **Best pick**: All-round assistant with agent mode. *Free plan + paid.*
2. [Claude](https://claude.ai): Long documents, writing and careful reasoning. *Free plan + paid.*
3. [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com): Assistant tied into Google Workspace. *Free plan + paid.*
4. [Manus](https://manus.im): Autonomous multi-step task agent. *Free plan + paid.*
5. [Grok](https://grok.com): Assistant with real-time X data. *Free plan + paid.*
6. [Hob](https://hob.chat): Free, open-source (MIT) desktop app that runs open-weight models locally and offline on Mac, Windows and Linux. *Free.*
7. [Omnara](https://omnara.com): Open-source, self-hostable managed-agent platform (alternative to Claude Managed Agents); $0 platform fee, pay as you go for managed models and machines. *Free plan + paid.*
8. [Wakeshift](https://wakeshift.ai): Wake sleeping AI agents (e.g. Claude Code) with events from your app; free up to 50,000 deliveries a month. *Free plan + paid.*
9. [telemetry.dev](https://telemetry.dev): OpenTelemetry-based tracing, cost and latency for LLM apps and agents; free 30k ingestion units a month. *Free plan + paid.*
10. [agent-qa](https://vostride.com): Web and mobile QA through CLI/MCP with memidex persistent memory, validated self-improvement and reviewable evidence. *Free local package; model costs extra.*

## Recent releases

- **2026-11-10**: Google Gemini 4 Carbon now available: Google made Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest model for long-horizon agent work, complex coding and debugging, scientific reasoning and cybersecurity, available beyond its earlier cyber-defender preview. Google positions Argon as the default for most work and Carbon for the hardest tasks (it uses more tokens). API in Google AI Studio and Gemini for business for paying developers at an introductory $2 / $10 per million input/output tokens until 30 November 2026, then $4 / $20; rolling out to Google AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search and Antigravity, which also previews a /swarm multi-agent feature. Benchmark rankings in the post are Google's own claims.. ([source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/))
- **2026-11-10**: Claude Whiteboards and Sheets (beta), Claude Data GA: Anthropic added Claude Whiteboards (a shared board where Claude groups ideas and drafts timelines and action items) and Claude Sheets (spreadsheets with tabs, formulas, formatting and drop-downs that Claude builds and edits, with export to .xlsx/CSV) in beta, and made Claude Dashboards generally available as Claude Data (certified queries and live sharing in beta; needs a data connector). All on paid plans, on web, desktop and mobile. Anthropic calls Sheets' functionality simple for now and says Claude Meetings is coming soon.. ([source](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data))
- **2026-11-10**: OpenAI: Businesses in ChatGPT (beta): OpenAI opened Business Hub in beta: eligible businesses can claim and verify their profile in ChatGPT, update details and product feeds with prices and availability, and see aggregated insights such as mentions, website clicks and discovery topics (no user conversations are shared). Eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses can also build a Business Agent that customers can chat with from the business profile; it can be sponsored as a ChatGPT Ads destination, with sponsored placements labelled. Shopify merchants can connect from Business Hub or the Shopify admin.. ([source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt))
- **2026-11-09**: Claude Fable 5.2 (and Mythos 5.2 for vetted orgs): Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2, available to all users on its apps and the Claude Platform (claude-fable-5-2) plus AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, with Mythos 5.2 (the same model with fewer cyber/bio restrictions) only for vetted organisations. Anthropic says it improves coding, reasoning and vision and writes up to 30% faster than 5.1. API price drops to $8 / $40 per million input/output tokens, with an optional fast mode (up to 2.5x faster) at $16 / $80. Benchmark claims are Anthropic's own.. ([source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2))
- **2026-11-09**: Meta Muse Ember: small, cheap model for agent work: Meta introduced Muse Ember (muse-ember-1.0), its smallest and most efficient Muse model, aimed at repeated agent tasks such as tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction and long-document parsing. It takes text, images, audio and video, has a 1M-token context window and up to 512K output tokens, and costs $0.10 / $0.50 per million input/output tokens on the Meta Model API and in Muse Code. Comparisons with other models are Meta's and Artificial Analysis's numbers.. ([source](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-ember))
- **2026-11-09**: ByteDance Seedream 5.5 image model: ByteDance Seed introduced Seedream 5.5, its next image generation model. Headline changes: more stable multi-turn editing that keeps unedited areas unchanged (shown across 100 rounds of edits), native transparent-background (RGBA) output so assets need no background removal, and more realistic images with clearer small text in English and Chinese. Quality comparisons are ByteDance's own evaluations; the post does not list where or at what price it is available, and a Pro version is still to come.. ([source](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/more-realistic-images-more-stable-editing-across-100-rounds-introducing-seedream-5-5))
- **2026-11-06**: Cursor: realtime voice chat with agents: Cursor added two-way voice calls with its coding agents in the Agents Window, the IDE, the web and Cursor for iOS. You describe a change out loud, ask what an agent is doing, and hear short progress notes while it works. Voice chat runs on Grok Voice with several voices to pick from, and runs with zero data retention when Privacy Mode is on. It is separate from dictation, which still turns speech into text.. ([source](https://cursor.com/changelog/voice-chat))
- **2026-11-06**: Google Antigravity adds voice mode and cloud Remote Agents: Google's Antigravity coding agent now has a voice mode, built on Gemini Live, in the Antigravity App and CLI 1.5.0+: you talk to the agent, steer it while it runs and answer permission prompts by voice, though destructive actions still need an on-screen click. Remote Agents, rolling out gradually to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers on personal accounts, run tasks in a Google-managed cloud environment that keeps working when your laptop is closed, using attached GitHub repos, Drive files or uploads.. ([source](https://antigravity.google/blog/antigravity-voice-mode/))
- **2026-11-06**: Meta brings Active Memory and Active Focus to Muse and Meta AI: Meta is rolling out specialized models, trained using its Muse Wonder series, to people who use Muse and Meta AI. Active Memory helps the assistant keep what matters across conversations and spot stale details (Meta reports 2.4x less forgetting of critical information), and Active Focus helps it keep working toward a goal when something goes wrong (Meta reports a 67% improvement on everyday tasks with unexpected problems). These are Meta's own numbers.. ([source](https://research.meta.ai/blog/specialized-models-for-muse-and-metaai))
- **2026-11-05**: Thinking Machines Inkling 2 Small (weights promised): Thinking Machines Lab introduced Inkling 2 Small, a mixture-of-experts model with 594B total and 17B active parameters that takes text and images and supports variable effort. It is available now on Tinker for sampling and fine-tuning with closed weights; the lab says it intends to publish full weights under Apache 2.0 after external safety testing, but they are not out yet. It does not support audio input.. ([source](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-small/))
- **2026-11-05**: Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash with 15-second video and conversational editing: Google DeepMind released Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash and made it generally available on the Gemini API and Google Cloud. Clips go up to 15 seconds (from 10 on Omni 1.1), with one-second steps from 3–15s, multi-turn conversational editing of the same clip, stronger image/video/audio reference adherence, and a 30% price cut to $0.07 per second for 720p. Drafts can start at 360p or 720p and upscale to 1080p or 4K. It rolls out in Flow, Google Vids, the Gemini app (Ultra), Google Ads, and Gemini for business; outputs carry SynthID.. ([source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/))
- **2026-11-05**: Claude Personal Finance (US beta): Anthropic is rolling out Personal Finance in Claude as a US-only beta on Pro, Max, and Ultra. You connect bank, card, loan, and investment accounts through Plaid with read-only access, then ask Claude about balances, spending, and upcoming bills in everyday language. Claude does not move money, sell financial products, or show ads. Account data in the Personal Finance hub is not used to train models; you can unlink accounts anytime.. ([source](https://claude.com/resources/articles/introducing-personal-finance-in-claude))
- **2026-11-05**: Cursor: build and deploy live apps from cloud agents: Cursor’s Nov 5 changelog adds live apps built by cloud agents: describe the app and Cursor scaffolds it, can provision Postgres and Cursor SSO auth, runs security checks, and deploys a shareable link on a domain you pick. Available on all paid plans with cloud agents enabled; team admins can review apps and secrets, approve blocked publishes, and unpublish. Individual plans can also share apps publicly.. ([source](https://cursor.com/changelog/build-live-apps))
- **2026-11-05**: Meta Muse Voice and expressive avatars (US rollout): Meta is rolling out real-time voice and expressive avatars in the Muse app in the US on mobile, web, and Mac. Tap the phone icon to talk; Muse Realtime Voice and Muse Realtime Avatar keep conversations fluid with interruptible replies and expressions that match the tone. You can use voice only, avatar beside chat, or full screen, and pick from hundreds of voice styles. Voice on AI Glasses and Muse Charm is coming later.. ([source](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/introducing-muse-ai-voice-expressive-avatars/))
- **2026-11-04**: Kimi K3.1 Pro open weights on Hugging Face: Moonshot AI made the full weights of Kimi K3.1 Pro public on Hugging Face (moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro). It is a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 2.8T total and 104B active parameters, a 1M-token context window, and text and image input, shipped in MXFP4. It uses Moonshot's own Kimi K3.1 Pro License rather than a standard open-source license, and the hosted API stays available on platform.kimi.ai.. ([source](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro))
